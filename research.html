Research

Our projects combine physical model tests, numerical simulation, and machine learning. Choose an area to filter the list.

Floating breakwater with integrated wave and tidal energy

We designed a 1:10 physical model of a floating breakwater that carries an oscillating water column and a horizontal axis tidal turbine. We are also developing a machine learning surrogate to identify suitable sites in Singapore waters.

  • Floating structures
  • Ocean energy

Reinforcement learning control of tidal turbines

We built a simulation environment for training and testing tidal turbine controllers. Agents learn pitch control under combined wave and current loading.

  • Ocean energy
  • Learning and control

Cyclic local buckling of steel columns

We simulate inelastic cyclic local buckling in steel hollow section columns under earthquake loading with a fiber based approach.

  • Seismic steel structures

Multihazard fragility of offshore photovoltaic platforms

We assess the multihazard fragility of offshore photovoltaic platforms with OpenSees models.

  • Floating structures

Compact material models for concrete filled steel tubes

We distil a trained Gaussian process surrogate into closed form expressions that give material model parameters for concrete filled steel tube columns.

  • Seismic steel structures
  • Learning and control

Seismic behavior of additively manufactured steel columns

A test program on the cyclic behavior of steel columns produced by wire arc additive manufacturing.

  • Seismic steel structures

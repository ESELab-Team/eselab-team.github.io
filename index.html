Seismic performance testing of 3D-printed steel columns begins

Seismic performance testing of 3D-printed structural steel columns has officially begun! This research is being conducted in collaboration with Professor Zhiyang Xie, Dr. Ting Lou, and Dr. Jialiang Jin. We look forward to a smooth test program that will deepen our understanding of the seismic performance of 3D-printed steel structures. As AI develops rapidly, reliable experimental data uncontaminated by AI are becoming especially valuable. We hope this program will produce high-quality data, and we will do our best to make the experimental data openly available.

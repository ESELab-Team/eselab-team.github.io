Emerging Structures and Energy Laboratory

We develop emerging structures, advanced numerical simulation methods, and new devices for energy harvesting. Our work supports coastal protection, clean energy, and the seismic safety of buildings.

Research areas

Floating structures

Wave attenuation, hydrodynamic response, and multihazard fragility of floating breakwaters and offshore platforms.

Ocean energy

Tidal turbines and oscillating water columns integrated into floating platforms.

Learning and control

Surrogate models, physics informed neural networks, and reinforcement learning control for marine and structural systems.

Seismic steel structures

Cyclic local buckling, fiber modeling, and collapse simulation of steel and composite members.

Shiye Wang

Research Fellow, National University of Singapore

Shiye Wang is a Research Fellow at the Coastal Protection and Flood Resilience Institute, National University of Singapore. Shiye received a PhD in Structural Engineering from Tongji University in 2024, including a year of joint training at EPFL.

Current research covers floating structures for coastal protection, tidal energy, reinforcement learning control, and the seismic behavior of steel and composite structures.
